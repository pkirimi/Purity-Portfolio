ABOUT ME

I build with AI, but I start with the problem.

I am an AI Engineer interested in the intersection of technology, product thinking and real world problems.

01 My approach

Technology should create useful outcomes.

I am interested in building AI and machine learning systems that are useful beyond a technical demo.

My approach starts with understanding the problem, the people affected by it and the outcome that matters. From there, I choose the simplest technical approach that can solve the problem effectively.

I care about the entire path from problem definition to working product: data, models, APIs, interfaces, deployment and the experience of the person using the system.

02 Background

A clinical background shaped how I think.

Before moving deeply into AI engineering, I worked in clinical medicine. That experience taught me to approach problems through evidence, context and consequences.

I now apply that same problem solving mindset to technology while building my engineering skills in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Healthcare remains an important part of my experience, but my engineering interests extend beyond healthcare. I am interested in using AI wherever it can solve meaningful business and user problems.
