# Purity Kirimi | AI Product Engineer Portfolio

## About

This is my personal portfolio website. It is designed to show employers, collaborators, and other visitors who I am, what I build, and the projects I have worked on.

The portfolio brings together my professional introduction, selected projects, case studies, background, and contact information.

## Portfolio Structure

The site is organized around four main areas:

Home → Selected Projects → About → Contact

The homepage introduces my professional focus and directs visitors to my work or contact information.

The projects section shows selected work and case studies. My case studies use a simple structure: the problem, what I did, and what came of it.

## How to View

The portfolio is publicly deployed and can be opened directly in a web browser.

For a local copy:

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open the main website page in a browser.

## Example User Journey

A visitor can:

1. Open the homepage and understand my professional focus.
2. Explore my selected projects.
3. Read a case study to understand the problem, my approach, and the result.
4. Read the About section to learn more about my background.
5. Use the Contact section to get in touch.

## Design Decisions

I chose to make my projects and case studies central to the portfolio rather than presenting only a list of skills.

I wanted visitors to see evidence of what I have built and understand the thinking behind the work.

I also used a simple page structure so that visitors can move from my introduction to my work and then to my background or contact information without having to search for these sections.

## Testing and Evaluation

I tested the portfolio beyond the normal happy path.

I checked the contact form, navigation, project links, CV, mobile layout, SEO, performance, accessibility, and best practices.

During an earlier mobile test, I found that some text on the About page was being cut off and the layout was too wide for the screen. I adjusted the responsive CSS and tested the page again.

## Limitations

The portfolio is still a work in progress.

Accessibility is one area that can be improved because the latest evaluation scored 90 rather than 100.

The portfolio will also need to grow as I complete more projects. Stronger and more detailed case studies will make the portfolio more useful over time.

## How I Used AI

I used AI as a collaborator while developing the portfolio. I used it to help with ideas, structure, writing, debugging, and refinement.

I reviewed the outputs, tested the website myself, and made the final decisions. I did not treat AI generated output as automatically correct.
