HerCycle Building a personalised women's health product.

I designed and built HerCycle as an AI powered women's health application that brings cycle tracking, reproductive health information and personalised experiences into one product.

ROLE Solo AI Engineer & Product Builder
PRODUCT Mobile AI Application
PLATFORM Android
STATUS Live product
01

The problem

Women's health is not a single journey. A person may move from cycle tracking to trying to conceive, pregnancy, postpartum, or later reproductive stages.

I wanted to build a product that could adapt to those different journeys rather than treating cycle tracking as the entire experience.

The product challenge was therefore not simply to build another period tracker. It was to create a coherent experience around different reproductive health needs while keeping the interaction simple enough for everyday use.

02

What I built

HerCycle was structured around multiple health journeys: cycle tracking, trying to conceive, pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause and exploring.

I also designed product features around personalised guidance, daily tracking and relationship support.

Personalised AI assistance Guidance designed around the user's current journey.
Cycle tracking Period setup and personalised cycle predictions.
Partner mode A way for partners to receive appropriate updates and provide support.
Journey based content Different experiences for stages such as pregnancy and postpartum.

05

Key decisions

Build a product, not just an AI demo

The AI component was designed as part of a broader product experience. Users still need onboarding, tracking, navigation, data storage and a useful interface around the intelligence.

Use journey based product design

Instead of creating one generic experience for every user, I structured the product around different stages and needs.

Keep the user in control

Health related information requires care. The product experience is designed to support users with information and tracking rather than pretending that an AI system replaces professional medical care.

06

What was difficult

Building the product involved more than writing application code. I also had to work through authentication, backend integration, mobile builds, app distribution and product publishing requirements.

I encountered issues around authentication behaviour, Expo builds, Google Play requirements, privacy documentation and application configuration.

Working through these problems gave me experience with the less visible parts of shipping an AI product: debugging systems, reading platform requirements, testing assumptions and getting a product from code to something users can actually access.

07

Outcome

The result was a working mobile product rather than a prototype limited to a design mockup.

HerCycle brought together multiple user journeys, personalised product experiences, data handling, AI capabilities and mobile deployment into one system.

I consider the most important outcome to be the experience of taking an idea through product design, engineering, debugging and deployment as a solo builder.
