Churn

Using customer data to explore who may be at risk of leaving.

A supervised machine learning project focused on customer churn as a business decision problem: identify patterns associated with customers who leave and create a basis for targeted retention analysis.

BUSINESS QUESTION Who is likely to churn?
PROBLEM Binary Classification
DATA Telco Customer Churn
USE CASE Retention decision support
01

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Losing customers is expensive.

Customer churn is not only a prediction problem. It is a resource allocation problem.

A business cannot intervene with every customer equally. A useful predictive system should help identify customers who may need attention so that retention teams can decide where intervention is worthwhile.

DECISION Which customers should receive retention attention first?
02

DATA EXPLORATION

Understand customer behaviour first.

The dataset contains customer-level information describing account characteristics, services, contract information and billing behaviour.

Customer profile Demographic and account characteristics
Services Products and services used by customers
Contract Contract and tenure information
Billing Payment and billing characteristics
03

MODELLING APPROACH

Predict risk, then interpret it.

I approached churn as a supervised classification problem. The objective is not simply to produce a churn label, but to understand which customer characteristics are associated with the prediction.

01 Inspect data
02 Prepare features
03 Train classifier
04 Evaluate predictions
05 Interpret customer patterns
04

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

Prediction only matters if someone can act on it.

A churn model becomes more useful when its output connects to an operational decision.

MODEL Churn probability
SEGMENT Customers requiring attention
ACTION Retention intervention
MEASURE Retention outcome

This framing shifts the project away from simply asking whether the model is accurate and toward asking whether its predictions can improve a business process.

05

WHAT I LEARNED

Machine learning should serve a decision.

This project helped connect technical machine learning work to a concrete business problem.

The interesting question is not simply whether a customer will churn. It is what the business should do with that information.

01 Define the business decision first.
02 Evaluate models using the right metrics.
03 Turn predictions into actionable segments.
